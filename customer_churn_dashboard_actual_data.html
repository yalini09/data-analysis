How to read the dashboard

Definitions for your analysis or presentation

Churn rate

Churned customers ÷ all customers in the selected group.

Retention rate

Active customers ÷ all customers. In this snapshot, it equals 100% − churn.

Revenue at risk

Sum of monthly charges for churned customers. It indicates exposure, not guaranteed lost future revenue.
